# Equity Research & Financial Valuation – Cisco Systems

## Overview

This project presents an equity research and financial valuation analysis of Cisco Systems (NASDAQ: CSCO) covering the 2021–2025 period.

The analysis evaluates Cisco's financial performance, capital structure, market performance and country risk, followed by an estimation of the company's Cost of Equity and WACC and two Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation approaches.

## Financial Analysis

The company is analyzed across several dimensions:

- Profitability
- Liquidity
- Leverage
- Financial performance
- Market performance
- Country risk

Historical financial statements are used to identify key trends and assess the company's financial position.

## Cost of Capital

The analysis estimates Cisco's Cost of Equity using the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM).

- Cost of Equity: **7.45%**
- WACC: **7.15%**

The WACC is subsequently used as the discount rate in the DCF valuation.

## DCF Valuation

Two valuation approaches are performed:

### FCFE Valuation

The Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE) approach estimates the value attributable directly to equity holders.

### FCFF Valuation

The Free Cash Flow to the Firm (FCFF) approach estimates the enterprise value of the company, which is subsequently adjusted for net debt to derive the implied equity value.

The resulting valuation range is:

**$120.6bn – $154.3bn**

This is compared with Cisco's market capitalization of approximately:

**$269bn**

## Key Takeaways

The analysis combines fundamental financial analysis with market-based valuation techniques to assess Cisco's financial position and implied equity value.

The DCF approaches produce an estimated equity value below the observed market capitalization, providing a basis for evaluating the difference between intrinsic valuation and market expectations.
